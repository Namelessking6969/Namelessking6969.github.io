My thoughts always seem to get the best of me. They loop over and over, pulling me into a place where everything feels heavier than it should. They make me feel alone, like I’m stuck in my own head with no real way out, like no one’s there to understand or help carry any of it.
Some days it’s overwhelming. The kind of weight that makes even small things feel impossible. I start questioning everything, and it gets hard to tell what’s real and what’s just my mind working against me. On those days, giving up feels like the easiest option—like maybe it would finally bring some kind of quiet.
Other times, it’s not even sadness. It’s just exhaustion. I get so tired of thinking, of feeling, of fighting my own head, that I just want to say fuck it and stop caring about anything at all..
